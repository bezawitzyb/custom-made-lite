Keep it as shown and I'll pick come first (version 1). Help me choose comes later, as its own test. Every event says which way the person chose (path), so each row below can be read on its own. The prototype records 21 events; the 22nd, order_completed, comes from the checkout.

Leaving, on every path

panel_closed is sent once when someone leaves the panel. It says which screen they left from (from_step, step_number), which way they chose (path) and how they left (reason). It is not sent after Add to bag, because that person reached the bag:

back_to_pagerestartleft_page

Together with step_viewed it shows exactly where each path loses people.

How to count

  • Count people, not events. Each time someone opens the panel they get one visit number (panel_session_id). Going back to a screen sends step_viewed again, so count visits, not views.
  • For each step, count the visits that reached it. The people lost at a step are those who reached the step before but not this one.
  • Someone who switches to Help me choose from the size screen sends path_selected with source: size. Keep them in the way they chose first, and count switches separately.
  • The suggested pair screen has no step_viewed. Use recommendation_shown to count who got there.

One naming rule

Every event is named "thing, then what happened", in the past tense, in lower case with underscores (panel_opened, product_added). Details like the feature and the test group go inside the event, not in its name. Nothing is recorded before the shopper agrees to tracking. Height and waist from Find my size are never sent with the shopping events.
